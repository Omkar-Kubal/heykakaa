## Goal today

Shape the app idea and get the first version down on paper.

## What I did

I came across Brainrot, an app on the App Store. I found its idea useful, but the experience felt more elaborate than it needed to be. It also made me feel guilty about my viewing habits instead of simply helping me understand them.

I started thinking about building my own Android version, with a simpler interface in the minimal style I’ve used in my earlier apps. I wrote down the problems I noticed and what I would want to do differently.

## Decisions + why

I decided to build the app myself. The idea felt useful, and I wanted to shape it into a simpler experience that helps people notice their habits without feeling guilty.

## Docs / assets

I started with handwritten notes, then used Claude Code to organize them into a clearer written app idea.
