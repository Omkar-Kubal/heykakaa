## Goal today

I wanted to give this app creation journey a home beyond short videos. The videos are where I can share a quick update each day; the website is where I can explain the work in more depth and keep everything together as the project grows.

## What I did

Today I created my website, bought a domain, and put [heykakaa.com](https://www.heykakaa.com/) online. It’s the place where I’ll document the process of building App #11, from the early idea through the decisions and work that follow.

I also set up the [Build Log](https://www.heykakaa.com/build-log). Each day’s entry has its own page, so you can watch a short video and then come here for the fuller story of what happened that day.

## Decisions + why

I’m using short videos for the quick version of the journey and this website for the in-depth version. A video can show the moment and the progress; a written entry gives me room to explain the thinking behind it and link the material I mention.

I want the documentation to be free to read. If you’re following the app or building something of your own, you can come back to any day without needing to pay or sign up.

## Learnings / blockers + Tomorrow

Getting the site online gives these updates one place to live. I can keep the videos short without losing the details that matter to the people who want to follow the entire process.
